## Missing categories and requests for Emily

Prioritize **a clean finished high-updo photograph** from rear/three-quarter view, plus a side view of a different updo. The spray/process shot is not used as a substitute for a finished result. Then supply a distinct half-up rear detail showing attachment and lengths; higher-resolution waves in even light; and examples on different hair lengths, colors, and natural textures where available. Sleek buns, short-hair styling, and distinct braid techniques are not established by this set.

Send individual photographer originals rather than screenshots/collage tiles, preferably at least 2400px on the long edge with room around the crown and ends. A larger original of the rear blonde-wave photograph and clean originals of the Caley/Lauren images are the most useful replacements. Include the style name, confirmation of which views show the same look, and available publication/photographer credit details. Additional wedding scenery is a lower priority than hair filling a meaningful part of the frame.
